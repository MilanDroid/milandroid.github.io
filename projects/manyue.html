Manyue

A ledger for a household that runs in more than one currency, kept up to date without the typing. Photograph a receipt, say one sentence, or hand it a bank statement — Manyue turns it into entries in the right account, under the right category. Receipts and statements arrive as drafts, and nothing from them is saved until you confirm.

Vue 3 TypeScript Fastify MySQL Gemini PWA English & Spanish

Every screenshot here is a fictional demo account: invented names and amounts, no real data.

The part nobody keeps doing

Budgeting apps mostly fail at the same point: the typing. A shoebox of receipts turns into an evening of data entry, the ledger goes stale, and the numbers stop being worth reading.

Manyue takes that step away. A photograph or a single sentence becomes an entry with a payee, a category and, where one shop was really two things, a split — and the ledger still balances to the cent.

Say it once

One sentence becomes a movement in the right account, on the right day, and when it cannot tell which account paid, it asks instead of guessing. The same box answers questions from your own ledger — what went to one payee, what still has no category — and can divide a bill between categories, set a card's limit, or move money between accounts and record what the bank kept.

The whole interface comes in English and in Honduran Spanish.

Every currency, honestly

Lempiras and dollars are counted apart and never added together, so no invented exchange rate sits inside the totals.

A card billed in two currencies keeps a balance in each, and its single credit line is worked out in both — so it can tell you how much is left on the dollar side. A transfer between currencies carries both amounts, and the fee the bank took.

Statements, compared before anything is saved

Drop in the bank's export — CSV, Excel or a statement PDF — and Manyue lines it up against what is already recorded: what is new, what is already there, what is probably there with the wrong date, and what is recorded but missing from the statement. Nothing is imported until you choose.

A spreadsheet is usually read without sending anything to a model at all. A PDF is read by Gemini in the background, and the page says so before you upload.

Transfers that were typed as two records

Money moved between your own accounts often gets typed as spending on one side and income on the other, which inflates both.

Manyue finds the likely pairs and turns each into the transfer it was, so it drops out of income and spending. A record that only looks like a transfer can be marked as not one, and a paired transfer can be split back apart.

The ledger

Under the shortcuts, an ordinary ledger that holds up to scrutiny.

On a phone

An installable web app with a tab bar and the assistant a tap away. What you record without a connection waits on the device and is sent when you are back.

How an entry gets in

Receipts and statements go through a draft you confirm. A sentence goes through the assistant, which asks rather than guesses. Typing it yourself skips both.

A photo is accepted at once and read behind the response, so a slow reading never holds the camera open.

Decisions worth explaining

Receipts and statements start as drafts

A receipt or a statement becomes a draft first. A model can misread a photo; it should never be able to change your balances on its own.

Ask, never guess

When the assistant cannot tell which account paid, it asks. An entry with a guessed field is worse than a question, because it is already done by the time anyone reads it.

Your receipts stay in your Drive

Photographs are kept in your own Google Drive and nowhere else. The ledger holds the numbers; the pictures stay yours.

Your own AI key, never a silent fallback

You can bring your own Gemini key. If it stops working, Manyue stops and tells you, rather than quietly switching to the shared key and billing someone else.

A shared key with a visible limit

Without your own key there is a daily allowance per person. The app shows what is left, and says “tomorrow” when it runs out instead of failing mid-scan.

Offline, without silent merges

What you record without a connection waits and is sent later. If it clashes with a change made elsewhere, you are shown both and you decide.
